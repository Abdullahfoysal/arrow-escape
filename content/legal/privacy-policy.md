**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Arrow Escape: Tap Puzzle** ("the game", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and readable. If anything is unclear, email us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

## Summary
- You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- Your progress, coins and settings are stored **on your device**. We don't run our own servers.
- We use **Google Firebase** to understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard), to fix crashes, and to adjust game settings without an app update.
- The game is free and shows **ads**. **Unity LevelPlay** picks which ad to show, and the ads come from **Unity Ads**, **Google AdMob** and other buyers that bid through them. These partners use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice later.
- Purchases are processed by **Apple or Google**. We never see your payment details.
- To ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion, email us your **Support ID** from **Settings › About** (see [Your choices](#your-choices)).
- The game is **not directed at children** under 13.

## Information we collect
**Information stored only on your device.** This is never sent to us: level progress, stars, coins, boosters, settings, your consent choices, the timing of the last ad (so ads aren't shown too often), and a short record of each purchase (product and transaction ID) so a purchase is never granted twice and Remove Ads can be restored.

**Information collected when you play.** This is sent to Google Firebase (our service provider) or to our ad partners:

| What | Examples | Why | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game usage | levels started, completed or failed; boosters used; coins earned or spent; screens opened; settings changed; sessions | Improve difficulty and the game (analytics) | Google Firebase |
| Crash and performance data | crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, app state at the time of the crash | Fix bugs and keep the game stable | Google Firebase Crashlytics. The ad partners' software also reports its own diagnostics to them. |
| Purchase information | product bought, price, currency, transaction ID | Revenue analytics. Delivering and restoring purchases is handled on your device and by the store. | Google Firebase (analytics). Apple or Google process the payment itself. |
| Device identifiers | identifiers created by Firebase for this installation of the game; your device's **advertising ID** (Android advertising ID; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking) | Analytics and crash grouping (Firebase IDs); showing, capping and measuring ads and preventing ad fraud (advertising ID) | Firebase receives only its own IDs. **Firebase does not receive your advertising ID.** Ad partners receive the advertising ID. |
| Approximate location | country or region worked out from your IP address | Choose the right consent message; ads and fraud prevention; analytics and game settings by country | Google (consent tool, Firebase); ad partners |
| Ad interactions | ads shown, clicked or watched; rewards granted; revenue per ad | Deliver rewards; measure ads; analytics | Ad partners; Firebase |
| Consent choices | whether you agreed to personalised ads or tracking, and your region | Respect your choices; show that we comply with the law | Stored on your device; a summary is sent to Firebase if analytics is allowed |

We do **not** collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, contacts, photos, videos, audio, files, precise GPS location, health data, messages, browsing or search history, or payment card or bank details. The game has no accounts, chat or social features.

We do not store purchase data beyond what the stores need to process and restore your purchases and what the game needs to avoid granting a purchase twice. We don't keep receipts or payment details.

## Consent, tracking and ads
- **Before anything is sent:** when you first open the game, Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) checks your region. Until that consent step is finished, the game sends no analytics, crash reports or ad requests.
- **Consent message:** if you're in a region where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK or Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent message. Game analytics then starts only if you allow it, and ads are personalised only if you agree.
- **After the consent step:** crash reporting and remote game settings start whatever you chose, because they keep the game working. Ads start after the consent step (and, on iPhone and iPad, after the tracking question).
- **Regions without a consent message** (for example the Philippines, Canada and New Zealand): analytics and personalised ads are on by default. You can limit ad personalisation through your device settings, as described below.
- **iPhone and iPad:** on the Home screen, after the consent message, we show a short explanation and then Apple's "Allow tracking?" question, once. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to us or our partners. The game works the same either way.
- **Changing your mind:**
  - In the game: **Settings › Privacy settings** reopens the consent message. This row appears only in regions where the consent tool offers it.
  - On iPhone and iPad: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  - On Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
- **Ads you'll see:** rewarded ads are optional; you choose to watch them for a reward such as extra coins or a hint. Interstitial (full-screen) ads may appear after you win a level, never during play or when you fail. Banner ads may appear on the Home and level-select screens. Buying **Remove Ads** removes interstitial and banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available.
- **Ad content:** we limit Google AdMob to ads rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) and below, and we monitor the ads shown through Unity's ad-quality tools. If you see an inappropriate ad, please tell us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

## How long we keep information
- **Analytics data** (Google Analytics for Firebase): event-level data is kept for **2 months**. Reports that only show totals and no individual player may be kept longer.
- **Crash reports** (Firebase Crashlytics): kept for **90 days**, then deleted.
- **Firebase installation IDs:** kept until we ask Firebase to delete them, which we do when you send a deletion request. Firebase then removes them from its systems within 180 days.
- **Ad partner data:** as described in each partner's privacy policy (linked above).
- **Data on your device:** until you uninstall the game.
